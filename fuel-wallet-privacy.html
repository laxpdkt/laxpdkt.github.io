Saka Fuel Wallet – Privacy policy

App by saka's corner (sakas.in). Last updated: 21 November 2026.

Your logs stay on your phone

Trips, daily logs, fuel fills, vehicles and settings that you enter are saved only on your own phone. We do not have a server for them, we cannot see them, and we do not sell or share them. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, they are deleted. The Back up button creates a file that you save or share yourself; where it goes is your choice.

Fuel prices

To show today's fuel prices the app downloads a public price file from https://sakas.in/prices.json. No personal information is sent with this request. Like any website, the hosting service (GitHub Pages) may record technical information such as your IP address in its own logs.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and information about ad interactions to show and measure ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads. See how Google uses information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Remove-ads subscription

Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card, UPI or bank details. Google tells the app only whether you have an active subscription. You can cancel any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

Children

This app is meant for adult drivers and vehicle owners. It is not directed at children under 13.
