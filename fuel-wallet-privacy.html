Saka Fuel Wallet – Privacy policy

App by saka's corner (sakas.in). Last updated: 21 November 2026.

Your saved trips and earnings stay on your phone

Trips, daily logs, fuel fills, vehicles and settings that you enter are saved only on your own phone. We do not have a server for them, we cannot see them, and we do not sell or share them. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, they are deleted. The Back up button creates a file that you save or share yourself; where it goes is your choice.

Location (trip meter)

If you use the trip meter, the app uses your phone's location to measure pickup km and trip km while a trip is running, including when you switch to another app such as Ola, Uber or Rapido or the screen is off. A "trip running" notification is shown the whole time. Only the distances and times are kept, on your phone; your location is never sent to us or anyone else. The meter stops when you end or discard the trip, or turn the Saka bubble off.

Display over other apps (Saka bubble)

If you turn on the Saka bubble, the app asks for permission to show a small button over other apps so you can start, board and end a trip without leaving your ride app. The bubble does not read or record anything shown by other apps.

Fare check (Google Maps)

When you tap Calculate fare, the start and destination you chose (or your current position, if you allowed location) are sent to Google Maps to work out the distance and travel time. Suggestions appear as you type a place name, so the letters you type are sent to Google too. Nothing else is sent: no name, phone number, trips or earnings. The app asks for location only when you tap "Use my location" or once you have already allowed it; without it you can type places or enter km and minutes yourself. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Fuel prices and wages

To show today's fuel prices and state minimum wages, the app downloads public files from sakas.in (prices.json, data/wages.json and data/app-config.json). No personal information is sent with these requests. Like any website, the hosting service (GitHub Pages) may record technical information such as your IP address in its own logs.

Phone backups and deleting your data

If your phone backs up apps to your Google account, the app's records are included only when that backup is end-to-end encrypted (your phone has a screen lock). The running trip and the app's internal security code are never backed up. To delete everything, use More → Your data → Delete all my data, or uninstall the app.

Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and information about ad interactions to show and measure ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads. See how Google uses information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Remove-ads subscription

Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card, UPI or bank details. Google tells the app only whether you have an active subscription. You can cancel any time in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

Children

This app is meant for adult drivers and vehicle owners. It is not directed at children under 13.
