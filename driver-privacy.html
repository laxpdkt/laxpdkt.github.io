Privacy Policy — SAKAS FairRate Driver

Last updated: 23 November 2026 · Developer: SAKAS (sakas.in) · Contact: support@sakas.in

Plain-English Summary: SAKAS FairRate Driver requires no login and no account. Your saved trips, earnings, fuel fills, and vehicle settings stay only on your phone. The app does not use Google Maps.

1. Your Data Stays on Your Phone

All personal driving records you enter or save in SAKAS FairRate Driver are stored locally on your device only (in local app storage under sakas-fairrate-driver-v1):

  • Your vehicles, mileage, fuel/EV prices, EMI/rent/lease, insurance, permit, and commission/subscription settings
  • Your saved trips, daily summaries, fuel fills, and odometer readings
  • Your optional driver name (used only when you create a PDF statement on your phone)
  • Your display, language, theme, and privacy (“Hide amounts”) preferences

SAKAS operates no user database and no backend server that receives your trips, earnings, fuel fills, or vehicle settings.

2. Internet Connections Made by the App

The app works offline for everyday calculations and logging. When connected to the internet, it contacts only the following services:

A. Read-only public price & wage files from sakas.in

The app fetches three public, read-only reference files over HTTPS (with no referrer and no personal identifiers):

  1. https://sakas.in/prices.json — public petrol, diesel, CNG, and Auto-LPG reference prices
  2. https://sakas.in/data/ev-tariff-tn.csv — public Tamil Nadu TNERC maximum domestic electricity slab rate for EV reference
  3. https://sakas.in/data/wages.json — verified public state minimum-wage notifications for drivers

B. No Google Maps

SAKAS FairRate Driver does NOT use Google Maps. It does not send pickup addresses, drop addresses, routes, or GPS coordinates to Google Maps or any third-party mapping service.

C. Advertising (Google AdMob) — Android Play Store build

To keep the app free for drivers, the Android app shows a banner ad served by Google AdMob (never shown on the Trip or Day entry screens, and removed completely if you buy a “Remove ads” subscription). Google AdMob may automatically collect device/advertising identifiers, approximate location (from IP address), app interactions, and diagnostics under Google’s Privacy Policy. Where required, an in-app consent form is presented first.

D. Optional “Remove Ads” Subscriptions (Google Play Billing)

Optional ad-free plans (₹10/month or ₹99/year) are billed and managed solely by Google Play Billing. SAKAS never sees or stores your card, UPI, or bank information.

3. Location & Notifications (Optional GPS Trip Meter)

  • Location and notification permissions are 100% optional. Calculators, manual trips, daily logs, fuel logs, and reports work without them.
  • In Auto Profit Tracker mode, the app shows an optional one-time GPS & Floating Bubble Permissions screen after initial vehicle setup (with a “Not now” button), and also asks right when you start a live GPS trip or enable the floating button.
  • Before asking for “While using the app” (foreground) location permission, the app shows a clear in-app disclosure.
  • On Android 13+, the app also requests Notifications so Android can display the persistent “Trip running” foreground status bar while GPS is active and gentle reminders if you forgot to end a trip.
  • No background location permission is requested, and GPS stops as soon as you end or discard the trip.
  • Your GPS coordinates stay strictly on your phone and are never uploaded to SAKAS or shared with third parties.

4. Display Over Other Apps (Optional Floating Bubble)

In the Android app, you may optionally enable “Display over other apps” for the small SAKAS floating button. It acts purely as a manual shortcut to open the quick calculator or start/advance/end your own trip timer. It does not monitor or read other apps’ screens or notifications, and no Android Accessibility Service or Notification Listener is used.

5. Backups, Exports & Sharing

  • Backup / Restore: JSON backups (sakas-fairrate-driver-backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json) are created on your phone and sanitized locally when restored.
  • CSV, WhatsApp & PDF Statements: Generated 100% locally on your device and shared only when you tap Share/Export.
  • Sample Data Mode: Example rides in Casually Exploring mode are stamped SAMPLE DATA and never modify your real records.

6. Deleting Your Data

Go to More → Your data → Delete all my data (confirmed twice) to erase all trips, days, fuel fills, vehicles, settings, cached reference files, and any running trip from your phone. Uninstalling the app also deletes all local data.

7. Children’s Privacy

SAKAS FairRate Driver is intended for adult commercial and gig drivers (18+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Independent Tool Disclaimer

SAKAS FairRate Driver provides independent cost and earnings estimates for driver awareness and personal financial planning only. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Uber, Ola, Rapido, Namma Yatri, Porter, or any ride-hailing or transport provider.
